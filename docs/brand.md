# Brand

**Who this page is for.** Anyone who shows the Tether name or mark: on a slide, a poster, a
paper, a web page or a tool that works with Tether. No repository access is needed to read
this page or to use the files.

The Tether mark is a single-molecule TIRF picture drawn as a letter T. The row of dots at the
top is the coverslip surface. The sizes of the dots follow the profile of the illumination
beam. Below the surface, three wavefronts go to one molecule. A faint grid of dots behind them
is the pixel grid of the camera.

## The mark

![The Tether mark in ink](assets/brand/mark-black.svg#only-light)
![The Tether mark in chalk](assets/brand/mark-white.svg#only-dark)

The mark has no background. Use the ink version on a light ground and the chalk version on a
dark ground. This page shows the version for the color scheme that you selected.

## The tile

![The light tile with the word TETHER](assets/brand/tile-light.svg)
![The dark tile with the word TETHER](assets/brand/tile-dark.svg)

The tile puts the mark and the word TETHER on a rounded square. Use the tile where the mark
must stand alone, for example on a title slide.

The application icon is the dark tile without the word:

![The application icon: the dark tile without the word](assets/brand/icon-dark.svg)

## The lockup

![The Tether lockup in ink](assets/brand/lockup-light.svg#only-light)
![The Tether lockup in chalk](assets/brand/lockup-dark.svg#only-dark)

The lockup puts the mark to the left of the word TETHER. Use it in a page header or at the
top of a document. The word is Red Hat Display Bold in capitals, converted to outlines. Do not
type the word again in a font. Use the files.

## Small sizes

A small icon cannot show all the detail of the mark. Use the version for the size:

| Size of the icon | Version |
|---|---|
| Up to 20 px | The tiny mark: three dots, two wavefronts. At 16 px, use the version that is fitted to the pixel grid. |
| 21 px to 32 px | The small mark: five dots, two wavefronts. |
| 33 px to 48 px | The mark without the pixel grid. |
| Larger than 48 px | The mark with the pixel grid. |

![The small mark on a light tile, at two times its size](assets/brand/icon-small-light.svg)
![The small mark on a dark tile, at two times its size](assets/brand/icon-small-dark.svg)
![The tiny mark on a light tile, at four times its size](assets/brand/icon-tiny-light.svg)
![The tiny mark on a dark tile, at four times its size](assets/brand/icon-tiny-dark.svg)

The small mark is shown at two times 32 px. The tiny mark is shown at four times 16 px.

## Clear space

Keep an empty area around the tile. On each side, the empty area is one quarter of the width
of the tile. Around the mark and the lockup, the empty area is one quarter of the height of
the mark. Put no text, line or other picture in this area.

## Colors

The brand is monochrome. It has no accent color.

| Name | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#121417` | The mark and text on a light ground |
| Paper | `#fbfbfa` | The light ground |
| Edge | `#d5dadd` | The edge of the light tile |
| Ground | `#0d0f12` | The dark ground |
| Chalk | `#f2f4f5` | The mark and text on a dark ground |

The colors of data in plots are not brand colors. The application keeps them separate.

## Type

The word TETHER is Red Hat Display Bold, in capitals, with wide letter spacing. The tagline is
"Provenance-first single-molecule FRET analysis". Red Hat Display is licensed under the SIL
Open Font License 1.1. Tether includes the font files without changes.

## Do not

- Do not change the shape, the proportions or the spacing of the mark.
- Do not add a color, a gradient, a shadow or an outline.
- Do not rotate, stretch, cut or crop the mark.
- Do not put the mark on a photograph or on a ground with low contrast.
- Do not use the mark with the pixel grid at 48 px or smaller.
- Do not use the tiny mark larger than 20 px or the small mark larger than 32 px.
- Do not put the mark on a figure of data. Tether puts no mark on the figures that it exports.
- Do not use the mark to say that the Tether project supports or approves your work.
